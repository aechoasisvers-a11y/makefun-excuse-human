One line, under 160 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline.

## Entry

> I delegated your deadline to a future version of myself, and he just ghosted us both.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
